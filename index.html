01 / Who We Are

Mokwepa is a 7-member academic–entrepreneurship group working at the intersection of mechatronics, automation & control, signal processing, and intelligent perception.

We develop prototypes, run experiments/simulations, and translate results into deployable solutions.

Research Themes

Control Systems & Autonomy

Design, tuning, and validation of controllers for stability, performance, and robustness.

Signal Processing & Sensing

Cleaning and extracting information from real signals (biomedical, industrial, and visual).

Intelligent Perception (Vision/OCR)

Computer vision pipelines and learning models for identification, localization, and extraction.

Industrial Digitalization

Dashboards and monitoring tools for operations, maintenance, and performance management.

Cross-Disciplinary Engineering

Mechatronic system design, integrating electronics, mechanical and software.

03 / Team

Structure & Operations

Mokwepa continues to operate as an academic–entrepreneurship group with 7 members. We organize work using roles that mirror real engineering teams, while allowing members to rotate and grow.

Roles

Project Lead / Systems Integrator

Coordinates scope, requirements, interfaces, and timeline. Ensures subsystem integration and end-to-end validation.

Control & Automation Lead

Designs controllers (PID/fuzzy/supervisory logic), simulations, and stability testing. Defines control metrics and tuning procedure.

Signal Processing & Data Lead

Owns preprocessing pipelines, feature extraction, and robustness analysis. Defines evaluation protocols.

Embedded & Hardware Lead

Implements sensing/actuation electronics, power architecture, and firmware logic. Handles reliability testing and hardware safety.

Mechanical & Prototyping Lead

CAD design, mechanism integration, mounting, tolerances, and manufacturability. Prototype build planning.

AI / Vision Lead

Owns vision pipelines (ROI detection, OCR training/inference), error analysis. Builds dataset strategy and model evaluation.

Software & Dashboard Lead

Builds APIs, UIs, dashboards, data storage/traceability layers. Ensures product usability and monitoring.

Documentation & Quality Lead

Maintains reports, versioning, formatting, and evidence pack completeness. Ensures consistent portfolio-quality outputs.

Operating Model

  • 01Research-first workflow: Problem → Prototype → Evaluate → Iterate → Document
  • 02Weekly cadence: short standups + a weekly evidence update
  • 03Validation mindset: every project includes at least one measurable metric and one failure case
  • 04Standardization: shared templates for reports, figures, naming conventions, and project pages
